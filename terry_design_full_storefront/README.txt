TERRY DESIGN FOOTWEAR — FULL FRONT-END STORE DEMO

Files
- index.html: page structure
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: interactive front-end behaviour

Run it
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open index.html using Live Server, or double-click index.html to open it in a browser.

Included
- Homepage hero and responsive navigation
- Product cards with sample NGN prices
- Category filters and product search
- Add-to-bag interactions, quantity controls, remove item, subtotal
- Checkout preview form (does not process payments or place real orders)
- Newsletter demo form
- Contact email placeholder

Important limitations
This is a front-end learning prototype. It has no backend, database, real customer accounts, stock tracking, order storage, email subscription integration, or payment processing. Do not use it to take real payments. To make it production-ready, the next phase would add a backend/API, database, secure authentication, real order workflows, and a trusted payment provider.
Product photography is loaded from Unsplash and needs internet access. Product names, prices, and contact details are examples and should be replaced with your real inventory and business contact.
